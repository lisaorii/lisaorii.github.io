LISA ORII

Human-Centered AI Researcher | Digital Transformation | Global Health

I design, develop, implement, and evaluate human-centered technologies in real-world contexts. I partner with end-users, designers, engineers, and domain experts, and apply qualitative and quantiative methods to identify and translate insights into design guidelines that shape the future of technologies. During my Ph.D., I led and supported digital health initiatives in Malawi’s and Kenya’s clinical contexts.

I received a Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from the University of Washington, where I was advised by Dr. Richard Anderson. I interned at United Nations Human Rights, OMRON SINIC X Corporation, and PATH. I received a Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science and Philosophy from Wellesley College.

In have been awarded MIT Technology Review Innovators Under 35 Japan (2022), the Quad Fellowship (2022), and the Funai Overseas Scholarship (2020).
