I design, develop, implement, and evaluate human-centered technologies in real-world contexts. I partner with end-users, designers, engineers, and domain experts, and apply qualitative and quantiative methods to identify and translate insights into design guidelines that shape the future of technologies. During my Ph.D., I led and supported digital health initiatives in Malawi's and Kenya's clinical contexts.

I received a Ph.D. in Computer Science &amp; Engineering from the University of Washington, where I was advised by Dr. Richard Anderson. I interned at [United Nations Human Rights](https://www.ohchr.org/en/ohchr_homepage), [OMRON SINIC X Corporation](https://www.omron.com/sinicx/en/), and [PATH](https://www.path.org/). I received a Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science and Philosophy from Wellesley College.
