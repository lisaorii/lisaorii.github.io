I design, develop, implement, and evaluate human-centered technologies in real-world contexts. I partner with various stakeholders, including governments, domain experts, and end-users, and apply qualitative and quantiative methods to identify and translate insights into design guidelines that shape the future of technologies. During my Ph.D., I led and supported digital health initiatives in Malawi and Kenya's clinical contexts.

I received a Ph.D. in Computer Science &amp; Engineering from the University of Washington, where I was advised by Dr. Richard Anderson. I received a Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science and Philosophy from Wellesley College. I interned at [United Nations Human Rights](https://www.ohchr.org/en/ohchr_homepage) (Geneva), [OMRON SINIC X Corporation](https://www.omron.com/sinicx/en/) (Tokyo), and [PATH](https://www.path.org/) (Seattle).
